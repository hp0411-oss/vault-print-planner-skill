## Where and how to share

1. Start with an existing VAULT owner community or the creator's community, if it permits third-party tools. Ask the moderator when the rules are unclear.
2. Use your own social account for the demo and a personal explanation of the problem it solves.
3. Check each community's current self-promotion rules before posting. Do not mass-post identical text or imply endorsement by VAULT / Bambu Lab.
4. Ask for specific installation/export feedback; avoid asking for stars as a condition of help.
5. Keep a small record of channel, date, feedback and fixes. Never publish testers' personal details without permission.

## First feedback round

Target: 3–5 volunteer testers. Useful outcomes: a clean installation, an identified parts list, a project opening in Studio, and a reproducible issue when something fails. Downloads do not prove successful installs; stars do not prove successful prints.

After fixes, publish a new version with concrete changes and verified platforms. Keep v0.1.0's original artifact intact.
